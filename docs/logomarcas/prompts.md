# Opções de logomarca — Mind Check

Quatro conceitos para um aplicativo de registro de pensamentos disfuncionais. Geração pela ferramenta integrada de imagens. São propostas visuais para escolha e refinamento.

## Diário e pensamento

Use case: logo-brand.
Create one original logo concept for an Android app called "Mind Check" that helps users record situations, automatic thoughts and emotions in a private thought diary. It relates to Registro de Pensamentos Disfuncionais in cognitive behavioral therapy.
Asset type: logo exploration, suitable for mobile app identity and university thesis.
Style: minimal flat vector-like graphic rendered as a raster image, clear outline or simple filled shapes, strong recognizable silhouette, friendly and calm rather than clinical. Professional understated design. No photographs, no 3D, no mockup, no shadows, no textures, no gradients, no tiny decorative details, no watermark.
Composition: one symbol above the exact wordmark "Mind Check" in clean readable sans-serif. Generous space between symbol and wordmark, centered square canvas with ample transparent margins. Symbol should remain recognizable at small sizes.
Background: genuinely transparent alpha background. No checkerboard pixels in artwork.
Do not use medical crosses, distressed faces, fragmented brains or symbols implying diagnosis. Use only the exact wordmark and no subtitle. Original imagery, avoid copying established brands.
Concept: a simple open notebook whose upper page outline smoothly forms one speech/thought bubble. Two short horizontal lines suggest written records on one page. Communicate capturing a thought in a diary. Palette: deep blue #294FBA plus soft blue #3768E8, wordmark dark navy #162033. Limit to these colors. One coherent icon, not two disconnected symbols.
